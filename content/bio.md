I am **Zihao Huang** (born in 1997), a Lecturer at the School of Environmental and Resource Sciences, Zhejiang A&F University (ZAFU), where I joined the faculty in July 2025. I received my B.S. degree in Geographic Information Science from Nanjing Tech University in 2018, followed by an M.S. in Forest Management (2021) and a Ph.D. in Forestry (2025) from ZAFU. From November 2024 to May 2025, I was also a visiting scholar at the University of Eastern Finland.

My research focuses on **spatiotemporal monitoring and simulation of land-cover change**, **forest resource remote sensing**, and **forest carbon cycle modeling**, with a particular emphasis on how land-cover evolution and forest age dynamics impact subtropical forest carbon cycles in China. My primary research line integrates multi-source remote sensing to reconstruct historical spatiotemporal dynamics of land cover and forest age, and couples process-based models with scenario simulations to project their combined effects on forest carbon storage and fluxes under future scenarios. Key technical methodologies include: (1) land-use/cover simulation models such as cellular automata and FLUS; (2) forest disturbance detection algorithms including LandTrendr and CCDC; (3) machine learning (e.g., RF, XGBoost) and deep learning (e.g., CNN, LSTM) methods; (4) ecological process models for forest carbon accounting, including BIOME-BGC and InTEC; and (5) spatial statistical and analytical models such as structural equation modeling and geographical detectors.

As of November 2026, I have published 7 peer-reviewed journal articles as first author in leading international journals, including **IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing**, **Agricultural and Forest Meteorology**, and **Geo-spatial Information Science**, with a cumulative IF exceeding **38**. I have also co-authored 38 journal articles in remote sensing, geosciences, and forestry. My publications have garnered **1,086** total citations (872 excluding self-citations) with an H-index of 21 (Web of Science). In terms of technical achievements, I hold **1 authorized Chinese invention patent as primary inventor** and 5 registered software copyrights.
