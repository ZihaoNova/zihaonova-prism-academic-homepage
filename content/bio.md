I am **Zihao Huang**, Ph.D., currently a Lecturer at the School of Environmental and Resource Sciences, Zhejiang A&F University. I received my B.S. degree in Geographic Information Science from Nanjing Tech University in 2018, and my M.S. degree in Forest Management and Ph.D. in Forestry from Zhejiang A&F University in 2021 and 2025, respectively. From November 2024 to May 2025, I was a visiting scholar at the University of Eastern Finland. I joined the faculty of the School of Environmental and Resource Sciences at Zhejiang A&F University in July 2025.

My research focuses on **spatiotemporal monitoring and simulation of land-cover change**, **forest resource remote sensing**, and **forest carbon cycle modeling**, with particular emphasis on how land-cover evolution and forest age dynamics impact subtropical forest carbon cycles in China. The main research thrust uses multi-source remote sensing to reconstruct historical spatiotemporal dynamics of land cover and forest age, and couples ecological process models with scenario simulation methods to project and investigate their combined effects on forest carbon storage and flux under future scenarios. Key technical methodologies include: (1) land-use/cover simulation models such as Cellular Automata and FLUS; (2) forest disturbance detection algorithms including LandTrendr and CCDC; (3) machine learning (RF, XGBoost) and deep learning (CNN, LSTM) methods; (4) ecological process models for forest carbon accounting including BIOME-BGC and InTEC; and (5) spatial statistical and analytical models such as structural equation modeling and geographical detectors.

As of November 2026, I have published 7 peer-reviewed journal articles as the first author in prestigious domestic and international journals, including ***IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing***, ***Agricultural and Forest Meteorology***, and ***Geo-spatial Information Science***, with a cumulative impact factor exceeding 38. I have also co-authored 38 journal articles in remote sensing, geosciences, and forestry. My publications have garnered 1,086 total citations (872 non-self citations) with an H-index of 21 (Web of Science). In terms of technical achievements, I hold 1 authorized Chinese invention patent as the primary inventor and 5 registered software copyrights.
